All your files in one place

Finding a file shouldn't be hard. File Explorer in Windows puts all your files and folders in one place. There, you'll find recommended, favourite and recent files you've used and the autocomplete feature that predicts your next moves to speed up everyday tasks.

Record what’s on your screen

If it’s on your screen, you can capture or record it with Snipping Tool and Screen Recorder. Just click, drag to frame your shot, and you’ve got it.

Connect your PC and your phone

Get seamless interactions between your mobile device and your Windows 11 PC with Phone Link. Connect your Android™ or iPhone® devices to view and manage notifications, send and receive messages, or make or take calls right from your PC.
